## RESIDENTIAL INTERIOR ARCHITECTURE
**Working title**: *Residences composed as slow, deliberate architecture.*

Caremanto Solutions designs private homes in Mumbai and across India as
**luxury residential interior architecture** — spatial commissions that
move beyond decoration into the deeper work of proportion, material and
light. Each residence is drawn as a chapter — hall and dining, bedrooms,
bathrooms, kitchens, and the quiet corners of balcony, dry area and
temple. Our craft standard is set at the join: stone against brass,
timber against travertine, daylight against shadow.

Deep expertise across **modular kitchen design, wardrobe joinery,
false-ceiling architecture, bathroom renovation, home renovation and
turnkey interior fit-out** — delivered as a single, calm commission.

## TURNKEY COMMERCIAL FIT-OUT
**Working title**: *Commercial space, measured with the same restraint.*

We deliver **turnkey commercial interior fit-outs** for offices, retail
and hospitality — three practices that share a discipline: the space
must earn its keep every day.

- **Office interior design** — headquarters, boutique studios, private
  workplaces. Composed for focus, choreography, and slow reveal.
- **Retail spatial optimisation** — flagships, showrooms and lifestyle
  boutiques. Product-first choreography, editorial materiality.
- **Boutique hospitality styling** — restaurants, cafés, private
  members' clubs, small hotels. Storytelling through service flow,
  material and light.

Every commission is engineered end-to-end — brief, drawings, execution,
site supervision, on-time handover.

## STRUCTURAL RENOVATION & HERITAGE RESTORATION
**Working title**: *Legacy, quietly re-composed.*

The Caremanto restoration practice handles **structural renovation,
adaptive reuse and heritage building restoration** across India — from
Mumbai bungalows to civic-scale interventions. We treat older
architecture as source material, not scaffolding to be replaced. Every
site brief begins with what stays. Every drawing begins with what can be
recovered.

Our restoration scope covers **façade conservation, structural
reinforcement, period detail reproduction, timber and stone
re-finishing, and modernised services integration** — carried out
alongside conservation architects and structural engineers when the
building demands it.
